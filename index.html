What I Offer

Services, laid out like a storyboard

Each frame below is a service I offer on its own, or as part of a full production from script to final render.

FR. 01

Character design

Original characters built from the ground up — silhouettes, turnarounds, expression sheets, and style guides ready for animation or merchandising.

FR. 02

Storyboarding & animatics

Shot-by-shot boards and timed animatics that lock pacing and staging before full production begins, saving time and budget downstream.

FR. 03

FR. 04

Guiding the visual language of a project across a team — palettes, mood boards, and style consistency from pre-production to delivery.

About

Hi, I draw things that move.

I'm an animator and character designer with a soft spot for hand-drawn line work and stories that feel a little bit alive. I trained in traditional 2D animation and spent a few years in studio pipelines before going independent to work directly with directors, small studios, and brands.

My favorite projects are the ones where a character shows up on page one as a rough scribble and leaves the process fully formed — with a walk, a voice, and opinions of their own. I care as much about the thinking behind a design as the polish on top of it.

When I'm not animating, I'm usually refilling a sketchbook, rewatching old Miyazaki films for the hundredth time, or looking for the next small, strange story worth telling.
